What is the 8-Puzzle?

Eight numbered tiles sit on a 3x3 board with one empty square. A move slides a neighbouring tile into the empty square, which is the same as moving the blank up, down, left or right. The task is to reach a goal arrangement in as few moves as possible.

As a search problem, each board arrangement is a state, each legal move is an action with cost 1, and the goal is a given arrangement. This app solves it with A* search, using the Misplaced Tiles or Manhattan Distance heuristic, and shows every expansion step by step.
